# Household Wealth Dashboard

A private, offline spending tracker and financial planner for your whole household.

**Your data never leaves your computer.** No server, no account, no internet needed.

## Getting started

1. Double-click `index.html`. That's the whole install.
2. Answer three quick questions: who's in your household, what accounts you have, and how you'd like to start.
3. Not ready to add your own numbers? Choose **Explore a sample household** to look around first.

On a phone or tablet, open the hosted link and add it to your home screen. See [DEPLOY.md](DEPLOY.md).

## Adding your transactions

Go to the **Import** tab and drop in a bank or card statement (CSV, PDF, or Word). Everything is read on your own machine. Nothing is uploaded.

You can also add transactions by hand, or try the example statements in `samples/`.

## What you'll see

- **Overview**: this month at a glance
- **Spending**: every transaction, searchable
- **Budget**: are you on pace this month?
- **Recurring**: subscriptions and bills
- **Cards & Credit**: utilisation, payment timing, a credit-factor scorecard
- **Wealth**: net worth, goals, and a debt-payoff planner
- **Plan & Advice**: your health score and prioritised next steps

## Back up your data

Your data lives in one browser on one machine. Go to **Import → Download full backup** once a month, and keep the file somewhere safe.

## A note on advice

The Plan tab applies general personal-finance guidelines to your figures. It doesn't know your tax situation or full circumstances and never recommends specific investments. For real decisions, talk to a licensed financial advisor.
